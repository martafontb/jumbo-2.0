About

An independent risograph studio and publisher. Small runs, made with artists, one colour at a time.

Est. 2019 · Canal Yard
01 — Our story2019 to now

Jumbo Press began in a shared unit at Canal Yard with one secondhand risograph and a stack of uncoated paper. We publish artists' books, zines and prints, and we print for people who want the same grain and layered colour. Every job is set up by hand, one drum per colour, tested on the paper you chose.
